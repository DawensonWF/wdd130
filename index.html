Port-au-Prince

Port-au-Prince, the capital of Haiti, is a lively city surrounded by mountains and the blue waters of the Bay of Gonave. Its busy streets, colorful markets, music, and local foods reflect the creativity and strength of its people. The city is home to historic landmarks, schools, shops, and communities with distinct character. Despite facing many challenges, Port-au-Prince remains an important center of Haitian culture and daily life. It is a place of energy, history, and hope, where tradition and new ideas meet. Its welcoming residents make every neighborhood feel unique, lively, and full of possibility for future generations to discover.
